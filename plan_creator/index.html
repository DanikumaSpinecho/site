Shortcuts

The planner is a desktop tool — it wants a mouse and some width.

Click a column headerselect it and list its abilities
Drag from the listdrop it on the column at that time
Drag a blockmove it in time, or to another column
Alt while draggingignore snapping
↑ ↓nudge by one snap step
Shift + ↑ ↓nudge by five steps
Delremove the selected block
Ctrl+Dduplicate it 30 s later
Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Yundo / redo
Escdeselect
Drag a column edgewiden it — double-click to reset
🔒 in the boss headerunlock to drag boss casts in time
☑ next to a jobuntick to hide that job's column
+2 on a role columnadd a column for another job of that role
▾ on a role columnmerge its jobs back into one column

Every job in the plan gets its own column. Hiding one only hides it — its mitigations stay in the plan and come back with the tick. A merged column carrying two jobs at once shows plain markers instead of icons: at that width nothing would tell you whose they are.
